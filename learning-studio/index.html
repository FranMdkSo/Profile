In a nutshell

A course made for you, delivered where you already are

MDK Learning Studio is my learning platform. You tell me what you want to learn, or what your team needs to learn, and I design a short, practical course built around your situation.

The course doesn't live on a platform you have to log into. It arrives on WhatsApp, like any other message, and moves at your pace: you read, you reply, and the next lesson arrives the following day.

How it works

Four steps, from idea to your hand

We talk

A short conversation: what you want to learn, why it matters and where you're starting from. If you have material (a PDF, some links, notes), send it over.

I make you a proposal

I come back with a course plan: the lessons, the questions and the rhythm. We adjust it together, and then I build it, using AI and checking every lesson by hand.

It arrives on WhatsApp

I send you a link, you send one message and you're in. One lesson a day, Monday to Friday, about five minutes. Reply whenever it suits you.

We talk about the result

I follow your answers and step in if something gets stuck. At the end we look at what changed: your confidence before and after, and what you'll apply.

Why it works

Small, spaced and conversational

It isn't a gimmick: the format is built on what research on learning has shown for decades.

🧩

Small pieces

One idea per lesson. When information arrives in small chunks, the mind processes it without overload.

📅

Spread over days

A little every day is remembered far better than one long session. That's the spacing effect.

💬

Learning by answering

Every lesson asks you to think and reply. Recalling what you learnt, rather than just rereading it, is what makes it stick.

📱

A channel you already use

Nothing to install, no passwords to remember. Messages get opened and read; training emails, not so much.

🔔

Gentle nudges

If a question goes unanswered, a single reminder arrives a few hours later. Just enough to keep the thread.

🎯

Made to measure

Examples from your world, in your language and at your level. Nothing generic.

🛠️

From knowing to doing

Each lesson points to something to try in real life. Learning lasts when it turns into action, not just awareness.

📈

Progress you can see

Confidence is measured at the start and at the end, so we both know what actually changed.

🤝

A person behind it

The messages are automated; the design and the follow-up are mine. More like a mentor who checks in than a catalogue.

How I design

The same principles I've applied in leadership and talent programmes across Europe, Asia and the Americas for more than fifteen years.

01

Start from the work

Every course begins with a real situation the learner faces, not with a list of content.

02

03

FAQ

What people usually ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you have everything. Open the link I send you, send the message that appears and you've started.

How long does a course take?

Usually one or two weeks, with one lesson per working day. We agree the length in the proposal.

What if I can't reply one day?

No problem. The lesson waits for you, and the next one doesn't arrive until you've answered. There's plenty of time to finish.

Am I talking to a person or a bot?

The lessons are sent by an automated assistant, but I design and review the course, follow your progress and can write to you directly when needed.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, Catalan, French and Dutch. Each course runs in the learner's language, and so does this platform: use the selector at the top of the page.

Does it work for a team or a company?

Yes. The same course can go to a whole group, with shared dates or individual start dates, and I can follow everyone's progress.

Where the idea comes from

Learning by messages

The psychology of learning proved this long before smartphones. More than a century ago, research on memory showed that we forget most of what we learn within days unless we come back to it, and that practice spread over time beats cramming. Later studies showed that recalling an answer fixes knowledge far better than rereading it.

Messages are simply the most natural way to bring those principles into everyday life: short, spaced and asking for a reply. MDK Learning Studio puts them to work at a human scale: one-to-one courses, designed for a specific person or group, by someone who has spent more than fifteen years in learning, leadership and organisational development.
