Talent, the currency of the future of work.

Optimistic and curious senior talent leader

FranMondaca

Global Learning • Talent • Organisational Development Leader

I design and scale learning, talent and organisational development that turns business priorities into stronger leadership, critical capabilities and internal career growth. Across healthcare, workplace technology and services industries, I connect workforce planning, skills, succession and governance, so that people are ready for what comes next and culture holds as the business scales. In the superworker era, that means moving from owning learning to enabling it, and building talent density rather than managing headcount. Platforms, technology and AI grow together, because everything starts with the business and ends with the business.
